About

I develop mathematical models for decisions under uncertainty, with applications in facility location, routing, supply chains, manufacturing, and maritime systems. My Ph.D. at NUS focused on data-driven robust optimization. My postdoctoral research at NUS focuses on maritime decarbonization and fleet transitions.

Selected projects

Facility location & routing

Data-driven robust optimization under spatial and travel-time uncertainty.

Research focus

My Ph.D. research developed distributionally robust facility location models for non-stationary spatial demand, with emergency-response applications, and robust pickup-and-delivery routing methods under uncertain travel times.

Supply chains & transport scheduling

Network design and integrated scheduling to improve logistics decisions.

Research focus

My work includes multi-objective supply chain network design considering risk and emissions, and integrated ship, stockyard, and train scheduling to reduce vessel delays.

Manufacturing & production planning

Optimization and learning methods for production systems.

Research focus

At A*STAR, I led modelling, simulation, and optimization for hyper-personalized manufacturing and developed production scheduling algorithms. Related published work explores neural networks for production planning automation.

Fleet renewal under fuel market uncertainty

Fleet investment under uncertain low-carbon fuel costs and supply.

Research focus

I model fleet renewal under uncertainty in low-carbon fuel costs and supply, assessing how alternative regulatory designs affect investment and operational compliance decisions. This SMI-funded research is associated with a paper under review at Transportation Research Part D, as listed in my CV.

More maritime research (3)

Economic impacts of maritime decarbonization

Assessing compliance markets, fuel rewards, and shipping competitiveness.

Research focus

I lead quantitative analysis and project coordination for Economic Value and Impacts of Maritime Decarbonisation Measures on Singapore. Earlier work on maritime mid-term measures quantified fuel costs, compliance-unit supply and demand, revenues, and fuel rewards, supporting MPA at IMO MEPC 83 in London.

Global maritime fuel and technology transitions

Simulation–optimization of vessel technologies, fuel mix, and emissions.

Research focus

I develop a simulation–optimization model of global maritime fuel and technology uptake, incorporating fuel switching, retrofits, newbuilds, and energy-efficiency measures. Dynamic cost–uptake feedbacks inform projections of fleet composition, fuel mix, and emissions pathways.

Singapore–Rotterdam maritime green corridor

Modelling the cost gap for corridor decarbonization.

Research focus

As a Research Fellow at NUS (July 2022–December 2024), I led cost-gap modelling and analysis, developing a long-term fleet-renewal model and coordinating delivery with port authorities, liner operators, and fuel suppliers. Findings were presented at consortium workshops in Rotterdam and Singapore.
